     1→     1→# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT — PS6: PLACEMENT HUB
     2→     2→## Build a Complete, Production-Structured, Full-Stack Evidence-Based Campus Placement Platform
     3→     3→
     4→     4→### 1. ROLE AND MISSION
     5→     5→
     6→     6→Act as a senior full-stack software engineer, solution architect, UI/UX designer, database engineer, AI engineer and application security specialist.
     7→     7→
     8→     8→Build a complete, functional, responsive web application called **Placement Hub**.
     9→     9→
    10→    10→This application addresses the following problem:
    11→    11→
    12→    12→College students often apply to many jobs using generic resumes and unverified skill claims. Placement officers receive openings through multiple sources, struggle to track candidates and duplicate postings, and lack a reliable way to rank students. Employers need shortlists based on demonstrable skills rather than unsupported resume statements.
    13→    13→
    14→    14→Your mission is to implement a unified evidence-based placement management platform that connects:
    15→    15→
    16→    16→1. Employer job requirements.
    17→    17→2. Students' actual skills and supporting evidence.
    18→    18→3. Explainable job-fit scoring and skill-gap analysis.
    19→    19→4. Truthful, job-specific resume generation.
    20→    20→5. Centralized application tracking.
    21→    21→6. Batch-wide student ranking.
    22→    22→7. Recruiter shortlisting and optional alumni and interview-question features.
    23→    23→
    24→    24→**Core product principle: Every skill claim, match recommendation and generated resume bullet must be traceable to supporting evidence or explicitly identified as unverified.**
    25→    25→
    26→    26→Do not build a generic job board with decorative AI features. Implement the actual business logic behind the product.

   540→   540→# 10. JOB-FIT SCORING ENGINE
   541→   541→
   542→   542→Implement an explainable and independently testable matching service.
   543→   543→
   544→   544→The system should compare job requirements with the student's evidence-backed profile.
   545→   545→
   546→   546→## Initial scoring formula
   547→   547→
   548→   548→For a given job and student, calculate:
   549→   549→
   550→   550→Fit score = 100 × (sum of requirement weight × evidence match value) / (sum of requirement weights)
   551→   551→
   552→   552→Use the following initial configurable weights:
   553→   553→
   554→   554→- Required skill: weight 4.
   555→   555→- Preferred skill: weight 1.
   556→   556→
   557→   557→Each requirement has an evidence match value between 0 and 1.
   558→   558→
   559→   559→An example policy:
   560→   560→
   561→   561→- No submitted supporting evidence: 0.
   562→   562→- Relevant evidence submitted but not yet reviewed: partial provisional credit.
   563→   563→- Relevant evidence verified: credit according to the configured verification and relevance rules.
   564→   564→- Rejected or irrelevant evidence: no positive credit from that evidence.
   565→   565→
   566→   566→These values are configurable policy examples, not universal measures of ability.
   567→   567→
   568→   568→When multiple evidence items support a skill, deduplicate the underlying skill contribution so one student cannot inflate their score merely by attaching numerous similar links.
   569→   569→
   570→   570→Keep the following concepts separate:
   571→   571→
   572→   572→- Skill claimed.
   573→   573→- Evidence submitted.
   574→   574→- Evidence verification status.
   575→   575→- Evidence relevance.
   576→   576→- Assessment result.
   577→   577→- Match score for a job.
   578→   578→
   579→   579→## Required skill handling
